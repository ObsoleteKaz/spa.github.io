About this site

Why this exists

September is Suicide Prevention Awareness Month — a reminder that a lot of people carry something heavy in silence, and that noticing, asking, and pointing someone toward help genuinely matters.

What this site is

A small, plainly-written resource about recognizing warning signs, supporting someone who's struggling, supporting yourself if that's you, and knowing where to turn. It was built as a design and development project, with the content kept as accurate and careful as possible.

What this site isn't

This is not a crisis service, a substitute for therapy or medical care, and no one is monitoring this page in real time. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, please use one of the hotlines on the Resources page or contact local emergency services.

On accuracy

The signs, conversation guidance, and myths & facts reflect widely-used public health guidance. Hotline details change over time — always verify a number directly with the organization before relying on it or publishing it further.

Who this is for

Anyone who's worried about someone, worried about themselves, or just wants to understand this better. There's no login, no tracking of what pages you read, and no expectation that you finish the whole site in one sitting.

Feedback

If something here reads as inaccurate, outdated, or unhelpful, that matters — content like this should be corrected quickly, not left to sit. Treat this as a living page, not a finished monument.

Built for Suicide Prevention Awareness Month. Not a substitute for professional care.
