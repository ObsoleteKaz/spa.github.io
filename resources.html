Where to go right now

This list is a starting point, not a complete directory. Hotline numbers change, and coverage varies a lot by country.

United States
988
Call or text, 24/7. Chat at 988lifeline.org. Veterans can press 1 after dialing.
United Kingdom & Ireland
116 123
Samaritans, free, 24/7.
Canada
988
Call or text, 24/7.
Australia
13 11 14
Lifeline Australia, call or text, 24/7.
Malawi
Local clinic or hospital
Ask at the nearest health facility for the mental health unit, or contact a local NGO working on mental health for a current crisis contact — check with them directly before publishing a number here.
Anywhere else
findahelpline.com
A directory of crisis lines by country, kept up to date by befrienders.org partners.

If you can't reach a hotline, a local hospital emergency room, a doctor, or a trusted faith or community leader can also connect you to help. If someone is in immediate physical danger, treat it like any other medical emergency and get them to care right away. Before this site goes live for real, verify every number directly with the organization that runs it — these change more often than people expect.
